## The wedge

The crowded entry in this hackathon is a dynamic or decaying **swap fee** that economically deters a dump. A determined seller pays the fee and dumps anyway — economics deter, they do not guarantee.

Latch is a different class. It changes **what the pool guarantees, not what it charges.** They tax the dump; we make the rug revert. Latch never touches the swap fee.

## Try it in two paths

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify a pool">
    Read `isVerified(PoolId id)` and `getEntry(PoolId id)` on `LatchRegistry` to confirm a pool is Latch Verified before you buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a pool">
    Call `LatchLauncher.launch(LaunchParams p)` — one transaction deploys the token, initializes the locked pool, seeds liquidity, and lists it in the registry.
  </Step>
</Steps>
